SPARKGRID

Sparkgrid is a memory game focused on renewable energy and sustainability. The goal of the game is to match pairs of related images. Some pairs show two versions of the same idea, while others show connected systems.

There are 20 cards total (10 pairs). Players flip two cards at a time and try to find matching pairs. The theme connects energy, water, air, and environmental systems that appear in everyday life.

Pairs in this game

  • Sun / Solar Panel
  • Wind Turbine / Power Tower
  • Water Filter / Water Drop
  • Electric Car / EV Charger
  • Energy Battery / Light Bulb
  • Smart Tower / Energy Gauge
  • Power Grid / Solar Roof
  • Carbon Dioxide / Leaf
  • Recycling Symbol / Planet Earth
  • Fuel Cell / Hydrogen

All icons were sourced from www.flaticon.com and resized to a consistent web-compatible format.
