Theme: Simulate

Three simple p5.js sketches that simulate creek water in different ways. Click a button to start or pause each sketch.

Sketch 1 — Gentle Flow

A simple flowing creek made from many short water lines moving to the right.

Sketch 2 — Flow + Leaves

A calm creek with water lines and a few leaves drifting across the surface.

Sketch 3 — Rain Creek

A faster creek with simple rain lines and quicker moving water.
